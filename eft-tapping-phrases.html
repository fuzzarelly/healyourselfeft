Most people know where to tap. They freeze on what to say.

So they say something vague, it never reaches what's underneath, and they decide tapping doesn't work for them. This script gives you every word, in the right order.

A peek inside

Four stages. 28 lines. Every word written for you.

  1. 1Name it

    Say what you're actually feeling, even the ugly bits.

    All this fear, all this worry, all this resistance.

  2. 2Accept it

    Stop fighting the feeling so your body can soften.

    I accept how I feel. It's ok to feel this way.

  3. 3Release it

    Give yourself permission to let it go.

    I'm open to the idea that I can let this feeling go.

  4. 4Settle

    Let your nervous system come back to rest.

    I allow myself to breathe with ease. I allow myself to rest.
